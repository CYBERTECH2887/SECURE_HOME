How it works

Three steps, and the first two take a minute each.

Add your people

Look at the webcam while SecureHome takes 50 photos. It only keeps frames with exactly one clear face.

Start the live camera

Known people get a green frame and are logged. Strangers get a red one with a timer.

Get the alert

After a stranger stays in view for {{ unknown_seconds }} seconds you get an email with their photo, and an entry in your dashboard.

Built to run at home

Offline recognition

ArcFace face embeddings and an SVM classifier run locally, and no images are sent to a third party. The first run needs internet once to download the face model.

Two-step matching

A face is classified, then checked against the stored faces of that person. This helps, but it is not perfect. See the limitations below.

One activity history

Every entry, alert and admin action is logged. Search it, filter it, and export it as a CSV file.

Private to you

Passwords are hashed, email codes expire, and each admin can only see their own photos.

Known limitations

Please read

SecureHome is a student project. This is what it cannot do well yet.

Works best with several people enrolled

With only one person enrolled, the system has nobody to compare against and can label every visitor as that person. Enrol 5 to 6 people or more to get a reliable split between known and unknown.

Lookalikes can be confused

Relatives, siblings and people who look alike may be matched to each other, especially with few enrolment photos.

Needs a clear, front-facing view

Face detection is the classic OpenCV Haar method. Side angles, masks, caps, sunglasses, dark rooms and faces far from the camera are often missed.

No protection against photo spoofing

There is no liveness check, so a photo or phone screen showing an enrolled person may be accepted as that person.

Only watches while the camera page is open

Monitoring runs while the server is on and the Live camera page is open. It is not a 24/7 background service.

One local webcam

It reads a webcam attached to the computer running the server. IP cameras, CCTV and RTSP streams are not supported.

Email alerts need internet and setup

Alerts and verification codes need a Gmail account with an app password and a working internet connection.

Speed depends on your computer

Recognition runs on the CPU. Older laptops may show a lower frame rate or a delay before a name appears.

Not hardened for the internet

It uses the Flask development server without HTTPS. Run it on your home network only, and do not expose it publicly.

A learning project, not a certified alarm

SecureHome is for study and demonstration. Do not rely on it as your only security system.
